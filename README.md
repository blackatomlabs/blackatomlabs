**Independent research and development laboratory · Spain**

> Build systems that know what to trust, what to forget, and how to prove what happened.

Black Atom Labs works on decisions that check coherence before they trust an input,
on state that can be revoked without losing its history, and on AI execution that
stays under human authority.

### Research lines

These are the lines the lab works on. Each one comes from problems found in
practice, not from plans.

| ID | Line | Status |
|---|---|---|
| RL-01 | Coherence-Aware Decision & Policy Systems | Principal line |
| RL-02 | Verifiable State, Revocation & Provenance | Principal line |
| RL-03 | Controlled AI Execution & Human Authority | Active research · early evidence |

### How work is labelled

Every piece of work published here uses the same two scales, so maturity and
openness are never mixed up: its maturity (research, prototype, reference
implementation, validated release, production, archived) and its exposure
(public, limited, private research). Internal evidence is never presented as
external validation, and reviews made with AI models are not independent audits.
